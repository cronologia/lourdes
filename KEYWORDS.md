# KEYWORDS — finding aid for Nossa Senhora de Lourdes

Naming variants for searching corpora, archives and the web. **Listing a term
is not asserting it** (sourcing-rules: absence is a claim). Searches for the
subject must try the accented and unaccented forms, and the pt-PT spelling
*Lurdes* alongside *Lourdes*.

## Portuguese (pt)

- Nossa Senhora de Lourdes
- Nossa Senhora de Lurdes / Nossa Senhora de Lurdes (pt-PT spelling; also the
  Brazilian toponym form, e.g. bairro de Lurdes)
- aparições de Lourdes
- gruta de Massabielle / gruta de Lourdes
- Santa Bernadete Soubirous / Bernadete / Bernardete Soubirous
- Bureau Médico de Lourdes

## Gascon Occitan (oc-gascon)

The phrase reported for the 25 March 1858 apparition — quote only AS REPORTED,
never as the site's own assertion. Spellings vary by source; search all:

- **Que soy era Immaculada Concepciou** (spelling used by the Sanctuary's
  English apparitions page, cited in the dataset)
- Que soy era Immaculada Councepciou (spelling common in French accounts)
- Qué soi era Immaculada Concepcion (normalized Occitan orthography)
- "Aquéro" — "that thing", Bernadette's own early name for the figure
  (appears in the interrogation records)

## Other load-bearing search terms

- Massabielle (also Masse-Vieille in some 19th-century texts)
- Soubirous (family name; beware OCR variants Soubiroux, Soubirons)
- Saint-Gildard / Espace Bernadette (Nevers)
- Dominique Peyramale (parish priest); Jacomet (police commissioner)
- Lourdes Medical Bureau / Bureau des Constatations Médicales /
  Comité Médical International de Lourdes (CMIL)

## Known traps

- *Lourdes* is also a common given name and toponym in Brazil, Portugal,
  Spain and the Philippines — a bare "Lourdes" hit is not necessarily the
  French shrine.
- pt-PT sources often write *Lurdes*; a zero for "Lourdes" in a Portuguese
  corpus is not a verified zero until "Lurdes" was searched too.
- The Gascon phrase is hard-wrapped or re-spelled in many sources; a phrase
  search that returns zero must be retried word-by-word.
